The structure La estructura · 2 fórmulas

✓ Affirmative
Subject+ have / has+ past participle
I have helped my neighbour.He ayudado a mi vecino.
She has cleaned the park.Ella ha limpiado el parque.
They have visited the hospital.Han visitado el hospital.
✗ Negative
Subject+ haven't / hasn't+ past participle
I haven't helped my neighbour.No he ayudado a mi vecino.
She hasn't cleaned the park.Ella no ha limpiado el parque.
They haven't visited the hospital.No han visitado el hospital.

Our verbs Los verbos de hoy · base → past participle

Regular · + ed

help → helped visit → visited clean → cleaned watch → watched play → played cook → cooked call → called open → opened finish → finished dance → danced study → studied

Irregular · only 5

buy → bought do → done make → made have → had go → gone

💡 Estos 5 no llevan -ed. ¡Apréndelos de memoria!

3

Think! Preguntas para comprobar · Concept check

“Maria has visited Cartagena.”

Did Maria go to Cartagena? (¿Fue María a Cartagena?)
Do we know when? (¿Sabemos cuándo?)
Is it an experience in her life? (¿Es una experiencia?)

“Carlos hasn't finished his homework.”

Is the homework ready now? (¿La tarea está lista?)
